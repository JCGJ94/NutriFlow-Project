# 3D icons

Icons from 3dicons.co by realvjy, licensed CC0 (https://github.com/realvjy/3dicons, "CC0-1.0 license"; the site states they can be used "on any personal or commercial projects without any attribution required"). Checked 2026-10-19.

The public previews have a white background baked in, so the background was removed locally (flood fill from the edges, 1px erode, feathered alpha) and saved as WebP with alpha. Used: bag, calendar, fire, gym, macros (chart), meals (tea-cup), rocket, setting, sun, target, trash-can.
